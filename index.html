Vacuum Rush

Legal and support information for the mobile game Vacuum Rush, published by João Pedro Teixeira Figueiredo.

In short: Vacuum Rush keeps your progress on your own device, has no accounts of its own, and sends no personal data to us. It is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob, to everyone; players below the age of digital consent only see non-personalised ads, and everyone else chooses personalised or non-personalised ads. On iOS, if you are signed in to Apple Game Center, scores and achievements go to Apple for the leaderboards. Read the details in the Privacy Policy.
