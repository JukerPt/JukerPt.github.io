Support

Questions, bug reports, privacy requests: write to shopglowave@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 14 days. Please do not send passwords or payment details; we never need them.

When you report a bug, include

  • your phone model and Android version (or iOS version),
  • the game version (shown in the game's settings),
  • what you did, what you expected and what happened, and a screenshot if you can.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone only. There is no account and no cloud save from us. (On iOS, if you are signed in to Apple Game Center, your leaderboard scores and achievements are also stored by Apple.) Uninstalling the game or clearing its data erases it, unless your phone's own backup restores it.

How do I delete my data?

Use the erase-data option in the game's Settings, uninstall the game, or clear its storage in your phone's app settings. We hold no game data about you on any server. Data that Google's ad software collected for ads is held by Google: reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings and manage ads at Google My Ad Center (see the Privacy Policy, section 10). Scores and achievements sent to Apple Game Center on iOS are held by Apple: manage or reset them in your device's Game Center settings or through Apple. If you emailed us and want that correspondence deleted, write to the address above.

Does the game track me or show ads?

It shows ads from Google AdMob, to everyone, but players below the age of digital consent (13 outside the EEA, UK and Switzerland, 13 to 16 inside them depending on the country) only see non-personalised ads. Google's ad software collects data such as your advertising ID, as described in the Privacy Policy. Players at or above that age choose personalised or non-personalised ads, and can change or withdraw your choice in Settings, under "Privacy choices" and "Ad privacy options". Players below it get no personalised-ads choice, and no tracking prompt on iOS. The game asks Google for non-personalised ads only. There is no other tracking, analytics or crash reporting. On iOS there is also optional Apple Game Center.
